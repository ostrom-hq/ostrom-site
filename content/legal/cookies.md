ostrom.run uses as little storage as it can. You choose, from the banner shown on your first visit or from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## Always on (no consent needed)

- **ostrom-theme** (local storage): remembers light or dark, if you picked one. Never sent to us.
- **ostrom-consent** (local storage): remembers your cookie choice for 6 months.

## Only with your consent

- **PostHog analytics** (local storage `ph_*`, no cookie; PostHog EU cloud): which pages are visited and how the site is used, so we can improve it. Kept 12 months. Not used for advertising, never sold.

## Changing your mind

Open "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page: refusing removes the analytics storage and stops the measurement right away. The console (app.ostrom.run) uses the strictly necessary cookies of your session; it has its own notice.
