Guimauve Digital SAS, 60 rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, France, is the controller of the personal data described here. For the data your organization puts in Ostrom Cloud (code, prompts, receipts, members), your organization is the controller and we process it on its behalf under our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa/).

## What we collect

- **Account data**: name, e-mail address, avatar and identifiers from GitHub, Google or your single sign-on, and the organizations you belong to.
- **Usage data of the service**: sessions, approvals, receipts, the rules applied, and technical logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) kept to secure and run the service.
- **Billing data**: the billing contact, the company name and address, the VAT number. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us.
- **Website analytics**: only if you accept analytics cookies, the pages you visit and how you use them, through PostHog (EU cloud). Without your consent, nothing is measured.
- **Messages you send us**: e-mails to hello@, security@ or legal@ostrom.run.

## Why, and on what basis

- To provide the service you signed up for (performance of the contract).
- To secure it, prevent abuse and keep accounting records (legitimate interest; legal obligation for invoices).
- To measure and improve the website (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page).
- To answer you (legitimate interest).

## Who processes it

We use these processors, each bound by a data processing agreement: Infomaniak (hosting, Switzerland), Cloudflare (DNS and content delivery), Stripe (payments), Resend (sign-in e-mails), PostHog (website analytics, EU), Grafana Labs (monitoring, EU), Langfuse (AI tracing, EU), GitHub and Google (sign-in). Some of them may process data outside the European Union; such transfers rely on an adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long

Account data: as long as the account exists, then 30 days. Technical logs: 30 days. Invoices: 10 years, as French law requires. Analytics: 12 months. Receipts: as long as your plan's retention.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its processing, restrict it, and withdraw a consent. Write to [privacy@ostrom.run](mailto:privacy@ostrom.run). You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
