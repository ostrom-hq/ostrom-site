This agreement is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) between your organization (the controller) and Guimauve Digital SAS (the processor). It applies when Ostrom Cloud processes personal data on your behalf.

## 1. Subject and duration

We process personal data to provide Ostrom Cloud, for as long as you use it, and for the 30 days of export that follow.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Hosting, storage and processing of the data your organization puts in the service: running agents, applying rules, asking for approvals, keeping receipts, sending notifications.

## 3. Data and people concerned

Members of your organization (name, e-mail, identifiers, actions), and any personal data present in the code, prompts, messages and documents your agents work on.

## 4. Our commitments

- We process the data only on your documented instructions, including these terms and your settings.
- People who access it are bound to confidentiality.
- We protect it with the measures described on our [security page](/security/): encryption in transit, isolation of each organization's data in the database, access on a need-to-know basis, logged.
- We help you answer the requests of the people concerned and with your impact assessments.
- We tell you without undue delay, and within 48 hours at most, after becoming aware of a personal data breach that affects you.
- At the end of the service, we delete the data after the export period, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- We make available the information needed to show compliance, and allow audits, at most once a year with 30 days' notice, at your cost.

## 5. Subprocessors

You authorize the subprocessors listed in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/). We tell you 30 days before adding or replacing one; you can object for a legitimate reason, and if we cannot address it, end the service and be refunded the unused part of a prepaid period.

## 6. Transfers

Data is hosted in Switzerland. A transfer outside the European Economic Area or Switzerland relies on an adequacy decision or on the standard contractual clauses.
