AI agents now write code faster than any team can review it. So companies face a bad choice: slow AI down, or lose control of it.

We're building the third option. Today we're opening Ostrom.

## What Ostrom does

Ostrom runs a company's AI coding agents in the cloud and makes them follow the team's rules. Every change they make leaves a receipt, and the few that matter wait for a person's yes.

- **See.** Every agent, every action, every dollar, visible.
- **Steer.** Your rules applied, your budgets, your approvals.
- **Prove.** A receipt for every action: what the agent was told, who approved it.

Ostrom reads your GitHub and proposes the rules your team already follows. Your agents run in the cloud under those rules, and when one wants to ship something important, the right person gets a message in Slack, with the proof, and says yes or no. Every change keeps a receipt.

## Few, targeted approvals

A yes asked everywhere becomes a reflex. Rules enforced by the system are followed far more often than rules written in a prompt, so Ostrom enforces them and asks a person only where it matters, with the evidence in view.

> Rules don't make agents smarter. They make agents follow the team.

## We run our own company this way

We are 4 people, with up to 20 agents working in parallel, and 142 projects run by agents. Speed is free now. Control is what's scarce.

## Open source, your models

Ostrom is open source, and every line of code is public. Companies pay per active person for our cloud, the enterprise features (unlocked by a key) and support. We never charge for the AI itself.

Bring your own models. We never resell model access.
