## Receipts

What a valid receipt guarantees:

- **Integrity.** Not one byte of the signed content changed since signing. Every field counts, including fields a verifier does not know.
- **Origin.** It was signed by the holder of the private key whose id is in the receipt.
- **Offline checking.** Anyone with the public key can check it, without asking Ostrom or the instance.

What it does not guarantee:

- **That the content is true.** A receipt records what the signing instance saw and decided. A compromised instance, or a stolen key, can sign false receipts.
- **That the key is the right one.** The verifier trusts the public key you give it. Get the key of your instance from a source you trust, and keep it.
- **Completeness.** A receipt proves what it contains. It does not prove that no other work happened without a receipt.
- **Secrecy.** Receipts are signed, not encrypted. Anyone who holds one can read it.

The test key of the [format's vectors](/docs/receipts/format#test-vectors) derives from a published seed. A receipt signed with it proves nothing.
