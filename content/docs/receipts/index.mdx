A receipt is a small JSON document, signed by an Ostrom instance, that says what happened during a piece of agent work.

A receipt records:

- what it is about: a session, a gate, a task or a workflow run;
- who started the work, and with which agent and model;
- which rules applied, each with the fingerprint of its exact text;
- every yes or no given, by whom and when;
- what it cost, when it is known.

## What a receipt proves

A valid signature proves two things: the receipt was signed by the holder of the instance's private key, and not one byte of it changed since. Change a name, a date or a cost, and the check fails.

It does not prove that what the receipt says is true beyond that. A receipt is as trustworthy as the instance that signed it and the safety of its key. See [security](/docs/security).

## Check a receipt

You need the receipt and the public key of the instance that signed it. The check runs offline: nothing is sent anywhere.

- By hand, in about 30 lines of code: [receipt format v1](/docs/receipts/format#verify-a-receipt-yourself) gives a complete verifier and the rules it follows.
- With `ostrom verify`: Coming soon, with the CLI.
